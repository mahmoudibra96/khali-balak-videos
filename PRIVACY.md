# Privacy Policy – khali-balak-stats

khali-balak-stats is a private tool used only by the owner of the "خلي بالك" YouTube channel (one user, the channel owner).

- It reads the owner's own YouTube channel statistics (views, watch time, subscribers) with read-only access.
- It uploads videos made by the owner to the owner's own YouTube channel, and sets their title, description, tags, audience setting and thumbnail.
- It never accesses, uploads to, edits or deletes anything on any other channel, and it has no other users.
- It does not collect, store, sell or share data about any other person. No YouTube data is shown to third parties.
- Access can be revoked at any time from https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

This tool uses YouTube API Services. YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms
